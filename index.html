Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VozaPay membership, earnings, and how the platform works.

How much does it cost to join VozaPay?

Joining VozaPay requires a one-time activation fee of ₦12,650. Members also receive a 100% cashback of ₦12,650 within the platform, allowing them to start participating in earning activities immediately.

How do I earn money on VozaPay?

Members earn through digital engagement activities such as watching videos, listening to audio content, downloading files, completing promotional tasks, and performing social media engagements.

Can I earn by referring others?

Yes. VozaPay offers partner bonuses when you invite new members to the platform. You can earn a partner bonus, reconnect rewards, and additional earnings from partner chain levels.

What activities can I complete daily?

Daily activities include video engagement, audio listening, file downloads, promo campaign tasks, and social media tasks. These activities allow members to earn consistently while engaging with digital content.

Who can join VozaPay?

VozaPay is designed for Africans, creators, and online users who want to turn their digital engagement into real earning opportunities.
